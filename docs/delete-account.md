# Delete your account

**ArmourUp Fitness**

## In the app

- Open ArmourUp Fitness and sign in.
- Coaches: go to **Settings**. Clients: go to **Profile**.
- Tap **Delete account**, type DELETE and confirm.

Your account and its data are deleted from our servers straight away. Accounts
that haven't subscribed or finished setting up also find **Delete account** on
the screen they land on.

## If you can't open the app

Email [henryarmour1@gmail.com](mailto:henryarmour1@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20account)
from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account".
We'll check the request comes from you, delete the account within 30 days, and
email you when it's done.

## What is deleted

- Your account and sign-in.
- Your training data: workouts, logged sets, notes, day types, custom movements,
  records and history.
- For a coach: every client on your roster, including their accounts, programs
  and history.
- Your notification settings and push tokens.
- Movement photos, which are only ever stored on your phone.

## What is kept, and for how long

- A short marker that each record was deleted (its ID and the time, with none of
  its content), so your other phones remove it too.
- A record that a notification was sent, for up to 30 days.
- A count of invite-code lookups against a scrambled network address, for up to
  1 hour.
- Notification delivery receipts, for up to 1 day.

## Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App
Store or Google Play account settings.
