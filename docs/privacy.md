# Privacy Policy

**Strength Coach**
Last updated: 13 September 2026

## The short version

Strength Coach keeps your training data in your account, so it is the same on
every phone you sign in on and a coach and their clients see the same program.
We use your data to run the app and for nothing else. There is no advertising,
no analytics, and we do not sell or share your data.

## What we store

When you create an account, and while you use the app, we store:

- **Your account**: your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by
  Google's Firebase Authentication; we never see them.
- **Coach details**: a coach's name, which their clients see.
- **Clients**: the name, email address, weight unit and invite code a coach
  enters for each client, and which account joined with that code.
- **Training data**: workouts, logged sets, weights, reps, session times and
  durations, coach notes, day types, the text of custom movements (name,
  description, cues and muscles), personal records and history.
- **Notification settings**: which notifications you have switched on, and a
  push token that lets us send them to your phone.
- **Subscription status**: whether a coaching subscription is active. Payment
  is handled by the App Store or Google Play; we never see your card details.

## What stays on your phone

- **Photos** you attach to a custom movement. They are copied into the app's
  own storage on your phone and are never uploaded.
- **Appearance**: light or dark.

## Who else handles it

A few services run the app for us. They process data on our behalf, not for
their own purposes:

- **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions)
  holds accounts and data, in the United States.
- **Expo's push notification service** passes notifications on to Apple's and
  Google's push services. It receives your push token and the text of each
  notification.
- **Apple and Google** process subscription payments and deliver notifications.

## What we don't do

- No advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
- We don't sell your data or share it for anyone else's purposes.
- The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or health
  data.

## Who can see what

- A coach sees everything about the clients on their roster: their details,
  workouts, logged sets and history.
- A client sees their own workouts, history and records, their coach's name
  and the notes their coach writes. Clients cannot see other clients.
- We can reach stored data only to operate and support the app.

## How long we keep it

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. A few records exist only
briefly, and are deleted automatically:

- a record that a notification was sent, so it is not sent twice: 30 days;
- a count of invite-code lookups, kept against a scrambled (hashed) network
  address rather than the address itself, to slow down guessing: 1 hour;
- a delivery receipt for each notification: 1 day.

## Deleting your account

Settings (coaches) or Profile (clients) → **Delete account** deletes your
account and your data from our servers immediately.

- Deleting a coach account also deletes that coach's clients' accounts, along
  with their programs and history.
- When a client deletes their account, their coach is told that they left.
- A short marker that a record was deleted (its ID and the time, with none of
  its content) is kept so your other phones remove it too.

Cancelling a subscription is separate: do it in your App Store or Google Play
settings.

## Exporting your data

Settings or Profile → **Export my data** gives you a copy of everything the
app holds about you, as a JSON file.

## Children

Strength Coach is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect data from them. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and
we will delete it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here and the date
above will change.
