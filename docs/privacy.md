# Privacy Policy

**Strength Coach**
Last updated: 8 September 2026

## The short version

Strength Coach keeps your data on your device. There is no server, no account
database and no analytics. The app makes no network requests of any kind, so
there is nothing for us to collect, sell or lose.

## What the app stores

Everything below is written to your device's local storage and nowhere else:

- **Your clients** — the names, email addresses and invite codes a coach enters
  when inviting someone, plus each client's chosen weight unit.
- **Training data** — programmed sessions, logged sets, weights, reps, session
  durations, coach notes, personal records and your training calendar.
- **Your own additions** — any custom movements you write, including
  descriptions, coaching cues and photos you attach from your photo library.
- **Preferences** — light or dark appearance, and which side of the app you are
  signed into.
- **Subscription status** — whether a coaching subscription is active. Payment
  itself is handled by the App Store or Google Play; the app never sees your
  card details.

## What the app does not do

- It does not transmit any of the above anywhere. There is no backend service.
- It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any
  kind, and contains no third-party SDKs that perform them.
- It does not create an account for you, and there is no password to lose.
- It does not access your location, contacts, microphone, camera or health data.

## Email addresses

A coach can enter a client's email address when creating an invitation. It is
stored on the coach's device as a label so they can tell two clients apart. The
app never sends email and never transmits the address. If you would rather not
record one, any text will do.

## Photos

If you attach a photo to a movement you have written, the app asks for
permission to read your photo library, and stores a reference to the image on
your device. Photos are not uploaded.

## Children

Strength Coach is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from
them, because it collects nothing from anyone.

## Your control over your data

- **Export** — Settings → Export my data writes everything the app holds about
  you to a JSON file you keep.
- **Delete** — Settings → Delete account removes your data from the device
  immediately and permanently. Because nothing was ever sent anywhere, deletion
  is complete at that moment; there are no copies to request the removal of.
- **Uninstalling** the app also removes everything it stored.

## A consequence worth knowing

Because your data lives only on your device and is never backed up to a server,
losing or wiping the device loses the data with it. Use Export if you want a
copy you keep somewhere else.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here and the date
