you were doing when it happened.

## Common questions

- **I forgot my password.** On the sign-in screen, type your email and tap
  Forgot password. We'll email you a link to set a new one.
- **My invite code doesn't work.** Codes are six characters and never contain
  the letters O or I or the numbers 0 or 1. If it still doesn't work, ask your
  coach for a new code: issuing one stops the old one working.
- **I want to cancel my subscription.** Subscriptions are billed by the App
  Store or Google Play, so cancel in your App Store or Google Play account
  settings. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel it.
- **I want a copy of my data.** Coaches: Settings → Export my data. Clients:
  Profile → Export my data.
- **I want to delete my account.** See [Delete your account](delete-account.html).
